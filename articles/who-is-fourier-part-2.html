Trigonometric Functions and Sine Waves

By Darshan · April 23, 2026

Trig functions. It's basically: if you take one side of the right triangle, call it theta, and make it so if theta changes, the other two angles change. Now, you might be saying, "Well, what's theta?" It's a side of a right triangle that isn't a right side.

Sine waves. A sine wave, or sin, is a wave that oscillates between positive and negative numbers and has a special formula.

f(θ) = sin θ

You can also add a number in front of sin to make the wave oscillate higher than 1, -1, like so:

f(θ) = 2 sin(θ)
