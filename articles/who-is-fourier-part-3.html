Wave Addition, Cosine Waves, and a₀

By Darshan · April 24, 2026

Wave addition is how you add waves: if you add however many waves that oscillate however many times, it will always repeat the same shape in x (the fundamental period).

It will always repeat the same shape in x — the fundamental period.

Now, cosine waves. Instead of sin θ = vertical/diagonal, cos θ = horizontal/diagonal. In simple terms: instead of being on the opposite side of theta, cosine is adjacent (horizontal).

Finally, a₀. a₀ gives us how to not oscillate with only 0 at the center. Like -2, 2, -1, 1, -5 all have a center of 0. Now we can just use a₀ for pairs like 5, 1, -3, 7, 6, 2.
